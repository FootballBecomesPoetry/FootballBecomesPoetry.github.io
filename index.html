About Me

Football has always been at the heart of my life. Growing up, I have experienced the highs and lows the game brings but always been captivated by the drama, skill and emotion that comes with it.

My love for football stretches far beyond the Premier League. It has led me to the most obscure games at the most obscure hours, and I would not have it any other way.

Alongside football, I have always been drawn to creative writing. There is something magical about turning feelings and experiences into words. Combining both felt completely natural.
